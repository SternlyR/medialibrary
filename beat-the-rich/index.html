The Show

The Concept

A high-energy game show at the intersection of financial literacy and entertainment — built for the generation that's done playing by the old rules.

Beat the Rich is a personal finance game show that transforms money education into must-watch content. Contestants compete through real-world financial challenges — budgeting rounds, credit battles, investment face-offs — with real cash on the line.

Hosted within the Hard Carry Media / Full Squad ecosystem, the show brings together a massive, highly engaged audience that is already primed to make moves with their money — they just need the right partner to do it with.

Part game show. Part financial education. Entirely entertaining. This is where culture and money finally meet.

  • 01
    The Challenge Contestants face real financial scenarios — budgeting, credit, savings — designed around the struggles everyday people actually face.
  • 02
    Beat the System Players go head-to-head, using financial knowledge and strategy to outmaneuver their opponents and the house.
  • 03
    The Payoff Real money, real tools, real takeaways. Winners walk away with cash and a clear path to financial freedom.
  • 04
    The Sponsor Integration Brand partners are woven organically into the gameplay — not ads, but a core part of the experience contestants actually use.
